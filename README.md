AniDeck is a Windows app that brings anime discovery, watching, tracking, and friends into one place—with a home screen you can make your own.

## Make it your deck

- **A home that fits you.** Arrange decks and widgets for your queue, tonight’s picks, quick progress updates, and shows you’re almost finished with.
- **Keep your progress in sync.** Connect AniList or Kitsu to manage your list and track what you watch.
- **Find your next watch.** Explore titles, browse recommendations, or let shuffle pick something for you.
- **Watch comfortably.** Episode navigation, subtitles, playback controls, and supported TV playback keep everything within reach.
- **Bring friends on deck.** Send messages and anime recommendations, compare tastes, and make watch plans together.
- **Your library, too.** Connect Jellyfin if you have one. It’s optional.

## Get AniDeck

**AniDeck is currently in development. A public Windows installer has not been released yet.**

When available, downloads will be posted on the [Releases page](https://github.com/Machoist/anideck/releases). The desktop app will not require Node.js, terminal commands, or hosting your own server.

## Getting started

Once you have the app:

1. Connect AniList or Kitsu in **Settings → Account** to sync your watch list.
2. Make Home yours by choosing your decks and widgets.
3. Open **Friends** to choose a handle and add people by handle or friend code.
4. If you use Jellyfin, connect your library from Settings.

Watch invitations let you agree on a title and time. Synchronized watch parties are not included yet. Streaming availability depends on the selected provider.

## Your privacy

AniDeck profiles are optional, and you choose who can see your watcher card. Only accepted friends can message you. Friends and messages are stored by AniDeck and are not end-to-end encrypted. Desktop message alerts are optional.
